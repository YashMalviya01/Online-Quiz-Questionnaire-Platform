Online Quiz Questionnaire Platform – Research References
Last updated: 2025-11-03

1. Alruwais, N., Wills, G., & Wald, M. (2018). Advantages and challenges of using e-assessment. International Journal of Information and Education Technology, 8(1), 34-37. https://doi.org/10.18178/ijiet.2018.8.1.1008
   Focus: Synthesizes benefits and barriers of e-assessment adoption, informing risk mitigation and feature prioritization for quiz delivery platforms.

2. Dermo, J. (2009). e-Assessment and the student learning experience: A survey of student perceptions of e-assessment. British Journal of Educational Technology, 40(2), 203-214. https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-8535.2008.00915.x
   Focus: Captures learner attitudes toward online assessment usability and fairness, guiding UX decisions and accessibility checks.

3. McDaniel, M. A., Agarwal, P. K., Huelser, B. J., McDermott, K. B., & Roediger III, H. L. (2011). Test-enhanced learning in a middle school science classroom: The effects of quiz frequency and placement. Journal of Educational Psychology, 103(2), 399-414. https://doi.org/10.1037/a0021782
   Focus: Demonstrates learning gains from frequent low-stakes quizzes, supporting scheduling and spacing features within the platform.

4. Stowell, J. R., & Bennett, D. (2010). Effects of online testing on student exam performance and test anxiety. Journal of Educational Computing Research, 42(2), 161-171. https://doi.org/10.2190/EC.42.2.a
   Focus: Evaluates performance and affective outcomes of online testing, informing analytics around student stress indicators.

5. Gikandi, J. W., Morrow, D., & Davis, N. E. (2011). Online formative assessment in higher education: A review of the literature. Computers & Education, 57(4), 2333-2351. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2011.06.004
   Focus: Reviews best practices for formative assessment, underpinning adaptive feedback and dashboard capabilities.

6. Cluskey Jr., G. R., Ehlen, C. R., & Raiborn, M. H. (2011). Thwarting online exam cheating without proctor supervision. Journal of Higher Education Theory and Practice, 11(2), 41-47.
   Focus: Discusses procedural and technical deterrents to cheating, influencing proctoring and integrity safeguards.

7. Rowe, N. C. (2004). Cheating in online student assessment: Beyond plagiarism. Online Journal of Distance Learning Administration, 7(2).
   Focus: Surveys cheating vectors specific to online exams, motivating audit trails and identity verification features.

8. Jordan, S. (2014). E-assessment: Past, present and future. New Directions in the Teaching of Physical Sciences, (10), 87-106. https://doi.org/10.29311/ndtps.v0i10.588
   Focus: Provides historical and future perspectives on e-assessment, guiding roadmap planning and standards alignment.
