Create a presentation for the Smart India Hackathon 2025 following the provided template and instructions.

Presentation Requirements
The presentation should have a maximum of six slides, including the title slide.

Use bullet points, diagrams, infographics, or pictures instead of paragraphs.

The explanations should be precise and easy to understand.

The idea must be unique and novel.

The presentation should be saved as a PDF file and uploaded to the portal. No other formats (like PPT or Word Doc) will be accepted.


The "Important Pointers" slide should be deleted before submission.

Slide-by-Slide Content
Slide 1: Title Page

Include the following details:

Problem Statement ID 

Problem Statement Title 

Theme 

PS Category (Software or Hardware) 

Team ID 

Team Name (registered on the portal) 

Slide 2: Proposed Solution

Title the slide "IDEA TITLE".

Describe your proposed idea, solution, or prototype.

Provide a detailed explanation of the solution.

Explain how the solution addresses the problem.

Highlight the innovation and uniqueness of your solution.

Slide 3: Technical Approach

List the technologies you plan to use, such as programming languages, frameworks, and hardware.

Detail your methodology and implementation process, using flowcharts, images, or a description of a working prototype.

Slide 4: Feasibility and Viability

Analyze the feasibility of your idea.

Identify potential challenges and risks.

Outline your strategies for overcoming these challenges.

Slide 5: Impact and Benefits

Describe the potential impact your solution will have on the target audience.

Explain the benefits of your solution (e.g., social, economic, environmental).

Slide 6: Research and References

Include details and links to your research and reference work.
