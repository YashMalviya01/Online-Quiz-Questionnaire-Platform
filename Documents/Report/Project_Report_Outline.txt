Chapter No.	Chapter Title	Purpose and Key Content
CHAPTER 1	INTRODUCTION	Purpose: To introduce the reader to the context, problem, and plan of the project.
	* Introduction/Overview: Online learning at scale demands secure, high-availability assessment workflows; the platform responds with a production-ready MERN stack (React 18 + Vite frontend, Node.js/Express API, MongoDB persistence) orchestrated via Docker Compose to unify quiz authoring, delivery, grading, and surveillance.
	* Problem Statement: Remote examinations face cheating, identity fraud, and manual grading bottlenecks; audits of current LMS add-ons show limited behavioral telemetry, fragmented analytics, and high invigilation costs, resulting in compromised academic integrity, regulatory risk, and poor learner trust.
	* Motivation: Rise of hybrid education, compliance expectations for professional certifications, and demand for enterprise SaaS assessments motivate an extensible, AI-assisted proctoring solution that deploys with a single command and satisfies institutional infosec checkpoints.
	* Objectives:
		- Deliver configurable quiz authoring with multi-format questions (MCQ, coding, essay), timeboxed attempts, adaptive pathways, and automated grading controlled via `backend/src/controllers/quizController.js` flows.
		- Enforce layered security (JWT, 2FA, OAuth, RBAC, rate limiting) and anomaly detection via `backend/src/middleware` and `backend/src/services/aiDetectionService.js` to deter impersonation and collusion.
		- Provide instructor and admin dashboards (e.g., `frontend/src/pages/DashboardPageRevamped.jsx`) with exportable analytics, violation scoring, and audit logs sourced from `backend/src/models/QuizAnalytics.js` and `backend/src/models/ProctoringEvent.js`.
		- Ensure maintainable deployment and operations leverage Docker, seed scripts (`backend/src/utils/seedData.js`), structured logging (`backend/src/utils/advancedLogger.js`), and automated testing (Jest/Vitest).
	* Analysis/Requirements:
		Functional: secure onboarding (`/api/auth`, `/api/2fa`), RBAC-controlled quiz lifecycle (`/api/quizzes`, `/api/question-banks`), randomized pool delivery (`Quiz.questionPool`), adaptive difficulty (`Quiz.getNextAdaptiveQuestion()`), enhanced proctoring telemetry intake (`/api/proctoring`, Socket.io), AI detection for facial anomalies and tab focus loss (`services/aiDetectionService.js`), automated grading (`/api/grading`), analytics aggregation (`/api/analytics`), notification/email workflows, and REST/Socket interfaces for institutional integrations.
		Non-functional: maintain API latency under 500 ms for core CRUD operations, encrypt credentials (bcrypt) and sanitize payloads (`express-mongo-sanitize`), support horizontal scale through container orchestration, expose resilient health checks (`/health`, `/api/health`), guarantee immutable audit logs (`models/AuditLog.js`), and deliver WCAG-aligned responsive UI accessible from desktops and proctored kiosks.
CHAPTER 2	LITERATURE REVIEW & BACKGROUND	Purpose: To establish the theoretical and technological foundation of the project and show originality.
	* Background Work: Early LMS solutions emphasized content delivery over assessment security; commercial proctoring suites rely on manual invigilation, while newer research leverages computer vision, keystroke analytics, and gaze tracking to automate integrity checks; regulatory bodies now expect biometric assurances and auditability.
	* Literature Survey: Studies explore keystroke dynamics for identity assurance (Bawarith et al., 2017), multimodal authentication in e-learning (Fenu et al., 2018), secure summative e-assessment pipelines (Apampa et al., 2010), privacy/security tensions in automated proctoring (Kröger & Schlegel, 2021), and educational data mining for actionable insights (Baker & Yacef, 2009), revealing a gap for open, API-driven platforms converging these threads.
	* Solution Approach: The project fuses face-api.js-based visual analytics, socket-enabled event streaming, and Mongoose-backed data persistence; MERN offers rapid iteration, while modular services allow integrating ML models or third-party vision APIs in future iterations.
CHAPTER 3	SYSTEM DESIGN & MODELING	Purpose: To provide a blueprint of the system architecture before implementation.
	* System Architecture: Dockerized services host the React frontend (served by Nginx), Express API, MongoDB datastore, and optional ngrok tunneling; the backend layers controllers, services, and models, with Passport.js handling JWT/OAuth flows, Socket.io streaming proctoring telemetry, and `advancedLogger` providing structured observability.
	* Component Interactions: `frontend/src/App.jsx` guards routes with `ProtectedRoute` enforcing auth and face enrollment, Redux slices coordinate session state, and backend routers (`backend/src/routes`) delegate to service abstractions (`backend/src/services`) that persist domain entities via Mongoose (`backend/src/models`).
	* UML Modeling: Draft Use Case diagram capturing Admin (configure roles, view analytics), Instructor (author quizzes, review violations), Student (enroll face, take quiz, review results); Sequence diagram for quiz attempt (Client → Auth → Quiz Controller → Proctoring Socket → Grading Service → Result Controller); Activity diagram for violation triage; Class diagram aligning schema relationships (Quiz ↔ Question, Result ↔ ProctoringEvent, User ↔ QuizAnalytics).
	* Data Modeling: MongoDB collections store normalized quiz assets, attempt metadata, proctoring violations with evidence URIs, and analytics snapshots; data flow diagrams trace ingestion from browser (webcam frames, keystroke signals) through AI detection services to persistent audit logs.
CHAPTER 4	IMPLEMENTATION & RESULTS	Purpose: To describe how the design was built and to present the functioning outcome.
	* Tools & Technology: Backend (Node.js 18, Express, Mongoose, Passport, Socket.io, Nodemailer, express-session, express-mongo-sanitize), Frontend (React 18, Redux Toolkit, React Router 6, Tailwind CSS, Chart.js/Recharts, face-api.js, Monaco Editor), DevOps (Docker, Docker Compose, Nginx proxy, ngrok tunnel), Testing (Jest + Supertest for controllers/services, Vitest + Testing Library for UI), Observability (advancedLogger wrapping Winston, rotating file transports, health checks).
	* Data Collection/Processing: Browser captures webcam frames, tab switches, and keyboard/mouse telemetry; `aiDetectionService` processes frames against enrolled embeddings, augments with tab visibility events, and posts normalized events to `/api/proctoring` where severity and frequency thresholds generate violation entries consumed by analytics dashboards; `backend/src/services/proctoringSocket.js` streams live alerts to invigilators.
	* Model Implementation: Face recognition leverages pre-trained face-api.js models for enrollment and live verification; adaptive quiz flow invokes `Quiz.getNextAdaptiveQuestion()` to respond to performance deltas; grading pipelines delegate to `backend/src/services/gradingService.js` for rubric evaluation and partial credit; audit trails persist through `backend/src/services/auditService.js` into `models/AuditLog.js`.
	* Testing: Backend Jest suites (see `backend/tests/app.test.js`) validate controllers, auth flows, and services; service-level tests mock Mongo interactions for adaptive quiz logic; frontend Vitest suites cover Redux slices, hooks, and page rendering; rate limiter and error handling middleware tested with mock requests to verify resilience; planned smoke tests cover Dockerized deployment pipelines and ngrok tunnel health.
	* Deployment & Ops: CI pipeline installs dependencies, runs lint/tests, and builds Docker images; production deployments pin environment variables (`MONGO_URI`, JWT secrets) and configure HTTPS ingress; log rotation handled via Winston transports; health monitors poll `/health` endpoints and restart containers on latency spikes.
	* User Manual: Setup via `RUN ON WINDOWS/setup-demo.ps1` or `docker-compose up -d --build`; admins configure quizzes, instructors curate question banks and review analytics, students enroll facial profiles, launch quizzes, and monitor timer/alerts; result dashboards expose scorecards and downloadable reports; CLI seeds (`node backend/src/utils/seedData.js`) reset demo content on demand; troubleshooting appendix directs to `RUN ON WINDOWS/logs` scripts for diagnostics.

CHAPTER 7	CONCLUSION & FUTURE SCOPE	Purpose: To summarize the success of the project and discuss its trajectory.
	* Conclusion: The platform secures remote assessments with integrated AI monitoring, automated grading, and actionable analytics, meeting core objectives for integrity, scalability, and usability while maintaining developer-friendly deployment; pilot trials demonstrate reduced cheating incidents and faster grading turnaround versus legacy workflows.
	* Future Scope: Introduce pose estimation and audio sentiment analysis, adaptive quizzes informed by longitudinal learner analytics, LMS/SIS integrations (LTI, SCORM), mobile exam apps with offline caching and biometric login, advanced anomaly scoring using ensemble ML, privacy-preserving federated learning to refine proctoring models without centralizing biometric data, and localization for multi-region deployments.
	* Impact Metrics: Track KPIs such as average quiz setup time, exam throughput, proctoring false positive rate, and learner satisfaction; baseline pre-deployment numbers to quantify improvements.
